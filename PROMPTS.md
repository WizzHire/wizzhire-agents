# Prompts to try

Copy any of these into Claude once WizzHire is connected (open [claude.ai/plugins](https://claude.ai/plugins), click **Add**, then **Add custom connector**, and paste `https://api.wizzhire.com/mcp`). They work with the connector alone; the [skills](README.md#hiring-skills) make Claude follow the same steps every time.

Replace the words in square brackets with your own. Claude understands English, Bosnian, Croatian and Serbian, and asks before it changes anything.

## Brand new account? Start here

A new account has no candidates yet, so start by creating something.

1. "Which WizzHire companies can you see, and what can you do in each?"
2. "Build a short screening form for a senior accountant role: six questions, with points."
3. "Now draft the job in WizzHire: senior accountant in Sarajevo, hybrid, two hires, applications open until the end of the month, with that screening form." (A form can be attached when the draft is created; for an existing job, attach it on the job's page.)
4. "Write interview questions and a scorecard for that job."

## Every day

5. "Who is waiting on me in WizzHire?"
6. "Tell me everything about the top candidate for [job]: fit, AI screening, answers and what the team said."
7. "Add a note on [candidate]: strong on accounting software, check the notice period."
8. "Rate [candidate] 4 out of 5."
9. "Star the three strongest candidates for [job]."

## Interviews and decisions

10. "Compare my starred candidates for [job] side by side."
11. "Here are my interview notes for [candidate]: [paste notes]. Turn them into structured feedback and save it on the profile."
12. "What has the team said about the shortlist for [job]? Who has not rated yet?"
13. "Ask [teammate] to rate [candidate] by Friday."

## Keep hiring moving

14. "Which candidates have been stuck in one stage for more than a week?"
15. "What is holding our hiring up, and who has to act?"
16. "What hiring tasks do I have today, and what am I waiting on from others?"
17. "Where do things stand with all our open roles?"

## Improve and report

18. "Check our [job] ad. Is anything missing that candidates look for?"
19. "Which channels brought candidates who got hired in the last 90 days?"
20. "Anyone in our talent pool for [job]?"

## What Claude will not do

Claude never moves, rejects or hires a candidate, publishes or edits a job, makes an offer or messages a candidate. It shows what it would change and waits for your yes. More: [docs/safety.md](docs/safety.md).
